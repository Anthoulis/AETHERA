Company profile

A family operation with an export mindset

From Kefalos, Kos, AETHERA brings together local knowledge, focused production, and the practical systems professional buyers need.

Our purpose is simple: to represent Greek provenance with honesty, present it beautifully, and build reliable long-term relationships around every shipment.

At a glance

Built around origin, clarity, and service

01

Company

  • Family roots dating to 1934
  • Based in Kefalos, Kos Island, Greece
  • Focused on thyme honey and extra virgin olive oil
  • Serving wholesale, retail, and hospitality partners
02

Production

  • Seasonal harvests shaped by local flora and conditions
  • Current honey capacity of approximately 2–4 tonnes per season
  • Retail and bulk formats coordinated per order
  • Typical lead times of 1–3 weeks, subject to stock and labelling
03

Quality control

  • Traceability by harvest and apiary location
  • Relevant laboratory analysis coordinated per batch
  • Unblended honey handled to preserve its natural character
  • Shipment documentation reviewed against agreed requirements
04

Partner support

  • Incoterms and delivery routes discussed per order
  • Palletised, protected loads prepared for dispatch
  • Multilingual labelling coordination where available
  • Prompt, direct communication with the wholesale team
